ABOUT

Our Heritage

Born from a Love of the Wild

Founded in 2018, Aetheria Haven began as a simple idea: that connecting with nature shouldn't require sacrificing the comforts of a luxurious stay. Our founders, avid outdoors enthusiasts with a background in luxury hospitality, set out to create a sanctuary where the rugged beauty of the wilderness meets thoughtfully designed accommodations.

Today, AETHERIA HAVEN spans over 500 acres of pristine forest, offering an unparalleled escape for those looking to disconnect from the noise and reconnect with the earth.

The AETHERIA HAVEN Way

More Than Just a Stay

Immersive Nature

Wake up to the sound of rustling leaves and flowing rivers. Every accommodation is positioned to offer maximum privacy and unobstructed views of the surrounding wilderness.

Curated Comfort

From 1000-thread-count sheets in our canvas tents to artisanal, locally sourced toiletries, we've thought of every detail to ensure your stay is as comfortable as it is adventurous.

Sustainable Ethos

We operate with a deep respect for the land. Our camp relies on solar energy, zero-waste practices, and a commitment to preserving the natural habitat for generations to come.

Our Lodgings

Where Comfort Meets Wilderness

Every stay at Aetheria Haven is a unique retreat designed to blend seamlessly into the environment while offering unparalleled comfort and luxury.

Luxury Canvas Tents

Experience the quintessential glamping experience. Our spacious canvas tents sit on raised wooden platforms and feature king-sized beds, cozy rugs, and private decks overlooking the river.

Modern Eco-Cabins

For those seeking solid walls and modern design, our eco-cabins offer floor-to-ceiling windows, private en-suite bathrooms, and climate control—perfect for year-round stays.

Vintage Airstreams

A nostalgic nod to classic Americana. These fully renovated Airstreams offer a retro aesthetic combined with modern conveniences like kitchenettes and plush seating areas.

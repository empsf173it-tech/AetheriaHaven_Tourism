Embark on unforgettable adventures and reconnect with nature.

The Great Outdoors

Adventure Awaits

Guided Hiking Trails

Explore miles of private, well-maintained trails ranging from easy walks to challenging ridge hikes. Our expert guides are available daily to share knowledge about local flora and fauna.

River Kayaking

Take to the water with our complimentary kayak and paddleboard rentals. The gentle river passing through our camp provides the perfect setting for a peaceful afternoon adventure.

Relax & Indulge

Wellness & Dining

After a long day of exploring, retreat to our outdoor spa for a restorative massage, or join our complimentary sunrise yoga sessions on the main deck.

When evening falls, gather around the communal lodge for farm-to-table dining. Our resident chef prepares seasonal menus over an open wood fire, creating a rustic yet refined culinary experience under the stars.

Plan Your Trip

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May

Wildflowers are blooming and rivers are rushing. Perfect weather for kayaking and photography.

Summer

June - August

Warm days and cool nights. Ideal for long hikes, swimming, and late-night stargazing.

Autumn

Sept - Nov

Crisp air and vibrant fall foliage. The absolute best time for hiking and campfire dining.

Winter

Dec - Feb

Quiet and snowy. A cozy time to retreat into a heated eco-cabin and enjoy the stillness.
